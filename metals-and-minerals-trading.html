Metals & Minerals Trading

JM International SPC’s trading division plays a vital role in the supply chain of Oman’s industrial and manufacturing sectors by sourcing and supplying high-quality metals and minerals. We connect global suppliers with local and regional buyers, ensuring transparent, responsible, and timely delivery of critical raw materials essential for economic development.

Products We Trade:
  • Base Metals: Copper, Aluminum, Zinc, Lead
  • Ferroalloys: Ferrochrome, Ferrosilicon
  • Industrial Minerals: Silica, Gypsum, Quartz
  • Scrap Metals & Recyclables: Environmentally responsible sourcing and resale
  • Construction Aggregates: High-grade materials for infrastructure projects
Our Trading Services Include:
  • Global Sourcing & Supplier Management
  • Import-Export Facilitation & Customs Clearance
  • Quality Control & Material Inspection
  • Logistics, Freight Forwarding & Delivery Coordination
  • Competitive Pricing & Contract Negotiations
Why JM International SPC for Metals & Minerals Trading?
  • Strong international supplier relationships
  • Commitment to ethical and sustainable sourcing
  • Transparent transactions and end-to-end service
  • Deep market insights ensuring cost-effective procurement
  • Supporting Oman’s industrial growth and sustainability targets
