Investment Director

Oman

Job Description

About the Role

JM International SPC is supporting a strategic leadership appointment for an Investment Director to lead a newly established Global Intelligence & Investment Office.

This role is intended for an international-caliber investment professional who will provide critical market intelligence and investment insight to support executive leadership and board-level decision-making across private and public equity investments.

Key Requirements

  • Senior experience in investment banking, private equity, sovereign wealth funds, family offices, or principal investing
  • Strong expertise in valuation methodologies, financial modeling, and deal structuring
  • Proven track record in cross-border and multi-jurisdictional transactions
  • Strong judgment and ability to operate independently in a senior advisory capacity
  • Excellent board-level communication, presentation, and stakeholder engagement skills

Responsibilities

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead global market intelligence efforts, monitoring macroeconomic trends, sector developments, and emerging investment opportunities
  • Screen and evaluate potential investments across private equity, public equity, and strategic assets
  • Oversee financial modeling, valuation analysis, feasibility studies, and investment memoranda
  • Conduct and manage commercial, financial, and strategic due diligence processes
  • Present investment recommendations, risk assessments, and strategic insights to executive management and board-level committees
  • Engage with external stakeholders including advisors, investment banks, fund managers, and partners across multiple jurisdictions
  • Support portfolio strategy, capital allocation decisions, and long-term investment planning
  • Establish governance frameworks, reporting standards, and analytical rigor for the Global Intelligence & Investment Office
